## Important capability boundary

Meta AI features vary by product surface, account, region, and rollout. Meta announced Muse Image in July 2026 and described Muse Video as a preview coming to creators and Meta AI; availability in a particular account must be checked in the actual interface. Meta has also described short-form AI video creation and remixing through Vibes. Do not assume every account can generate arbitrary-duration clips, maintain a character across many shots, produce Japanese voice acting, or export separate audio tracks. Verify what the current interface offers before planning around it.

Official references:
- Meta, *Introducing Muse Image and Muse Video*: https://ai.meta.com/blog/introducing-muse-image-muse-video-msl/
- Meta, *Introducing Vibes*: https://about.fb.com/news/2025/09/introducing-vibes-ai-videos/

**Production fallback:** if a requested video-generation or audio feature is unavailable, generate an approved still or short silent clip, then animate/edit it in a video editor. Record Japanese dialogue separately, add sound effects/music in the editor, and create Thai subtitles as an editable SRT file. Never mark a deliverable complete until the exported file has been checked.

## Prompt notes for key motion shots

### Shot 01-07/01-08 — Soul Ink surge
Use Rin and sanctuary references. Show the energy ring traveling across cracked ground, then a single outward shockwave that bends nearby branches and lifts petals. Keep Rin's silhouette readable; cyan energy may darken at the edges for one brief beat. Use anticipation → burst → falling debris/petals. Do not add a new creature or change Rin's design.

### Shot 02-05/02-06 — Blood Core barrier
Use Akane and Rin references together if multi-reference input is supported. Akane raises two fingers; mirror planes open; red crystal stakes rise and lock into a stable geometric perimeter. Cyan energy presses against the barrier but does not pass through. Keep crystal count and geometry consistent through the shot. The effect should feel precise and theatrical, not like an uncontrolled explosion.

### Shot 03-06 — Rin accepts Akane's hand
Use both approved character references. Compose a close insert of the hands first, then a medium two-shot. Rin accepts after a brief hesitation. Cyan and crimson reflections meet across the hands; the red barrier dissolves into a few sparks. Keep fingers anatomically consistent and clothing unchanged. No romantic framing; this is a wary pact between mentor and student.

### Shot 04-01 — Reveal Nebula Garden
Begin with the three silhouettes at the garden path and pull back through the fractured sky to reveal a gothic city, old theaters, dark towers, and blood-red neon. Preserve the city’s dark fantasy palette. Do not invent named landmarks or add readable signage.

### Shot 05-03 — Xeron's cliffhanger
Use Xeron's existing reference. Frame the gloved hand and printed image of Rin, not Rin herself. A dark-coated blade crosses the photo and splits the paper; cut to Xeron's yellow eyes. Keep the action stylized and non-graphic. Add the scrape and impact later in the editor. Do not rely on generated text or logos.
