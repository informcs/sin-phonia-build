## Logline
In the ruined Twilight Sakura Sanctuary, Rin Tsukiyomi struggles to contain a dangerous surge of Soul Ink. Kaguya Akane seals the outburst with Blood Core crystals and offers Rin a way out. A cheerful green-lit stranger joins them, while a watcher in a distant theater marks Rin as a new target.

## Main cast in this episode
- **Rin Tsukiyomi** — protagonist; cyan Eclipse Ink, volatile Soul & Eclipse power.
- **Kaguya Akane** — white nine-tailed fox demon; red/black outfit, Illusion & Blood Core.
- **Elvira Greenwood (disguised form)** — small silver-haired girl with green lantern; apparently helpful, secretly manipulative. Do not reveal her true identity yet.
- **Xeron Vax** — appears only in the final cliffhanger.

# Screenplay

## SCENE 1 — THE WITHERED ECLIPSE
**00:00–02:45 | Location:** The Fallen Sanctuary of Twilight
**Mood:** cold, uncanny, disoriented
**Sound palette:** wind through dead branches, distant layered whispers, low synth bass, sparse bells

### 1A. Opening image (00:00–00:35)
**VISUAL:** Black screen. A thin red crescent appears through a fracture in the sky. Slowly reveal a ruined sakura garden: cracked earth, a giant withered tree, faded petals drifting against the wind. Hold long enough for the audience to read the space.

**SFX:** Low wind, a faint crystalline creak, petals scraping across stone.
**MUSIC:** A low sustained synth note enters after the first few seconds; no melody yet.
**DIALOGUE:** None.

### 1B. Rin awakens (00:35–01:15)
**VISUAL:** Close on Rin’s fingers digging into the dry ground. Her eyes open sharply. Cyan light pulses beneath the Eclipse Ink on her forehead. She presses a hand to her head as overlapping whispers swell. Cut between her eyes, the mark, and branches trembling without being touched.

**RIN (whispering, shaken):**
「また……また聞こえる……頭の中の叫び声……やめて……止まってよ！」
**THAI SUBTITLE:**
“อีกแล้ว… ฉันได้ยินอีกแล้ว… เสียงกรีดร้องในหัว… พอได้แล้ว… หยุดสักที!”

**SFX:** Layered whispers move from left to right; a restrained heartbeat begins.
**PERFORMANCE:** Breath is uneven; fear is real, but Rin’s stubbornness is already visible.

### 1C. Soul Ink surges (01:15–02:05)
**VISUAL:** Rin tries to stand. Cyan Soul Ink spreads in a ring across the ground. A shockwave bends branches and sends petals spinning outward. The camera pulls wide to show how small Rin is against the dying tree. For a brief beat, the cyan aura darkens at the edges, hinting at the danger without fully revealing a monster.

**RIN (strained, partly to herself):**
「どうして今、ソウル・インクが目覚めるの……？　私が制御できなかったら……本当に、破滅の魂に呑み込まれるの？」
**THAI SUBTITLE:**
“ทำไม Soul Ink ถึงตื่นขึ้นมาตอนนี้…? ถ้าฉันควบคุมมันไม่ได้… ฉันจะถูกวิญญาณแห่งความพินาศกลืนกินจริง ๆ งั้นเหรอ?”

**SFX:** A low pulse, brittle branches snapping, whispers briefly forming an unintelligible chorus.
**MUSIC:** Bass rises, then cuts for half a second at the peak of the surge.
**ANIMATION NOTE:** Use anticipation → burst → delayed falling petals. Keep Rin’s face and outfit on-model.

### 1D. The garden distorts (02:05–02:45)
**VISUAL:** The horizon bends as if seen through hot glass. A few petals freeze in the air, then resume moving in reverse for one unsettling beat. Rin drops to one knee, trying to breathe. The cyan aura flickers toward black, but the scene stays readable.

**RIN (a breath, barely audible):**
「いや……まだ、終わりたくない……！」
**THAI SUBTITLE:**
“ไม่… ฉันยังไม่อยากจบลงแบบนี้…!”

**SFX:** Whispers collapse into a single low tone; the wind stops abruptly.
**TRANSITION:** A sharp red spotlight cuts down from above.

## SCENE 2 — THE VISITOR FROM THE DARK
**02:45–05:30 | Location:** Same garden, inside the distorted space
**Mood:** theatrical, dangerous, controlled
**Sound palette:** glass harmonics, cello, piano accents, restrained gothic orchestra

### 2A. Akane’s entrance (02:45–03:25)
**VISUAL:** A red beam strikes the ground like a stage spotlight. High angle: a luxurious floating throne forms from reflected fragments. Akane sits elegantly, white fox tails drifting behind her. Low angle on her crimson eyes and knowing smile. She studies Rin as if the chaos is an inconvenience—and an opportunity.

**AKANE (softly amused):**
「まあ……自分の影に喰われかけている迷子の子狼？　なんて愛らしい光景かしら。」
**THAI SUBTITLE:**
“อุ๊ยตาย… ลูกหมาป่าหลงทางกำลังจะถูกเงาของตัวเองกลืนกินงั้นเหรอ? ช่างเป็นภาพที่น่าเอ็นดูจริง ๆ”

**SFX:** One clear glass chime; faint crystal fragments rotate in the air.
**MUSIC:** Cello and piano enter with measured, theatrical phrasing.

### 2B. Rin pushes back (03:25–03:55)
**VISUAL:** Rin looks up through the unstable cyan glow. Over-the-shoulder shot from Akane’s elevated position; reverse close-up on Rin’s defiant eyes.

**RIN (weak, hostile):**
「お前……この狐女……何をする気……？」
**THAI SUBTITLE:**
“แก… ยัยจิ้งจอก… คิดจะทำอะไร…?”

**AKANE:** A small laugh, barely more than a breath.
**SFX:** A single tail-swish, cloth movement, crystal resonance.

### 2C. Blood Core containment (03:55–04:45)
**VISUAL:** Akane raises two fingers. Mirror-like planes open around the clearing. Blood-red crystal stakes emerge and lock into a precise geometric perimeter around Rin. Show three readable stages: crystals appear, rotate into position, then connect with red lines. Rin’s cyan aura pushes against the barrier; the barrier flexes but holds.

**AKANE (calm, matter-of-fact):**
「何って？　助けてあげているのよ。今ここで身体が壊れてしまったら、私が探している『種』まで台無しでしょう？」
**THAI SUBTITLE:**
“ทำอะไรน่ะเหรอ? ฉันกำลังช่วยเธออยู่ต่างหาก ถ้าร่างเธอพังทลายตรงนี้ ‘เมล็ดพันธุ์’ ที่ฉันตามหาก็เสียเปล่าน่ะสิ”

**SFX:** Crystal strikes, layered glass resonance, a deep containment thrum.
**MUSIC:** Orchestra swells during the lock, then settles once the field stabilizes.
**ANIMATION NOTE:** Do not let the effects obscure Rin or Akane. The barrier geometry must remain consistent across shots.

### 2D. Akane’s private calculation (04:45–05:30)
**VISUAL:** Close on the Eclipse Ink reflected in Akane’s eye. Cut to a wider shot: Rin kneels inside the red geometry while Akane’s throne slowly descends. A brief reflection of Akane’s smile appears in a floating mirror fragment.

**AKANE — INNER THOUGHT (voice-over, measured):**
「月蝕の刻印……想像以上に濃い。古い魂の器で間違いないわね。うまく育てれば、復讐のための切り札になる……。」
**THAI SUBTITLE:**
“ตราประทับจันทรา… เข้มข้นกว่าที่คิด เป็นภาชนะของวิญญาณโบราณไม่ผิดแน่ ถ้าฝึกให้ดี เธออาจเป็นไพ่ตายสำหรับการล้างแค้น…”

**SFX:** A soft mirror shimmer; the heartbeat in the mix slows.
**PERFORMANCE:** The thought is calculating, not openly villainous. Keep her public expression playful.

## SCENE 3 — AN OFFER BEHIND A SMILE
**05:30–08:20 | Location:** The containment field, then the edge of the garden
**Mood:** wary hope, banter, a hidden agenda
**Sound palette:** footsteps on cracked stone, green lantern hum, restrained synth pulse

### 3A. The offer (05:30–06:15)
**VISUAL:** The field dims. Rin sinks to the ground, breathing hard. Akane steps down from the throne; each heel click echoes. She offers her tattooed hand. Keep the space between their hands visible to build tension.

**AKANE (gentle, with an edge):**
「リン、私と来なさい。ネオンの街へ。そこで力の使い方を学ぶのよ……それとも、ここで一人きりで終わる？」
**THAI SUBTITLE:**
“ริน มากับฉัน ไปยังเมืองแห่งแสงนีออน ที่นั่นเธอจะได้เรียนรู้วิธีใช้พลังนี้… หรือจะจบลงอย่างโดดเดี่ยวที่นี่?”

**SFX:** Three measured heel clicks, then silence.
**MUSIC:** A subtle pulse returns, less threatening than before.

### 3B. Elvira appears (06:15–06:55)
**VISUAL:** A green glow peeks through thorny bushes. Elvira’s small disguised form steps into the edge of frame holding a lantern. Her silver hair catches the green light. She smiles brightly, apparently oblivious to the danger. Do not frame her like a revealed antagonist.

**ELVIRA (cheerful, overly sweet):**
「お姉さまの言うとおり！　私たちが『インク』を抑えてあげなかったら、青い髪のお姉さまは理性のない悪魔になっちゃうんだから！」
**THAI SUBTITLE:**
“พี่สาวพูดถูกแล้วค่ะ! ถ้าพวกเราไม่ช่วยสะกด ‘หมึก’ ไว้ พี่สาวผมฟ้าคงกลายเป็นปีศาจไร้สติแน่เลย!”

**SFX:** Lantern chime, tiny leaves rustling, a soft green magical hum.
**PERFORMANCE:** Bright and playful on the surface; no overt hint that she is manipulating them.

### 3C. Rin chooses (06:55–07:40)
**VISUAL:** Rin glances at Elvira, then at Akane’s outstretched hand. Her breathing steadies. She hesitates, then takes Akane’s hand. Cyan and crimson light reflect across their fingers. Elvira’s lantern adds a thin green edge to the frame.

**RIN (dry, defiant):**
「……わかった。でも、私を簡単に操れると思わないでよ、おばさん。」
**THAI SUBTITLE:**
“…ก็ได้ แต่ไม่ต้องคิดว่าจะควบคุมฉันได้ง่าย ๆ ล่ะ ยัยป้า”

**AKANE:** A quiet laugh, genuinely entertained.
**SFX:** Soft crystal release; the barrier dissolves into red sparks.
**MUSIC:** Synth pulse lifts into a forward-moving rhythm.

### 3D. The hidden thought (07:40–08:20)
**VISUAL:** The three figures begin moving toward the garden path. Keep Rin and Akane in the foreground; Elvira trails a few steps behind. Her public smile remains unchanged. For only a moment, the green lantern reflects in her eyes, then the shot cuts away before revealing more.

**ELVIRA — INNER THOUGHT (voice-over, playful whisper):**
「ふふ……そのまま進んで。私の描いた筋書きどおりにね……。」
**THAI SUBTITLE:**
“ฮิฮิ… เดินต่อไปแบบนั้นแหละ ทำตามบทที่ฉันวางไว้…”
**SFX:** A barely audible leaf-like rustle, tucked under the music.
**CONTINUITY:** This is foreshadowing only. Do not reveal the Core Seed or Elvira’s final-boss identity in Episode 1.
