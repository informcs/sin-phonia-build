## Animation strategy
- Generate short moving clips for high-value motion beats only: 01-07/08 (Soul Ink surge), 02-05/06 (Blood Core barrier), 03-06 (hand acceptance), 04-01 (city reveal), and 05-03 (photo cut).
- Use controlled camera moves, layered parallax, particle overlays, and held key art for quieter shots.
- Keep dialogue-heavy shots simple so lip-sync errors are less visible; use close-ups, over-the-shoulder framing, and reaction shots when necessary.
- Do not expect a single generative video prompt to preserve all characters, props, and geography for ten minutes.
- Record exact clip start/end handles and keep each selected take's source filename in the shot manifest.

## Review gates
1. **Design gate:** approve Rin, Akane, Elvira, Xeron against current references.
2. **Motion gate:** approve one 4–6 second test shot before generating the full episode.
3. **Continuity gate:** verify geography, costume, mark placement, crystal layout, and screen direction.
4. **Audio gate:** fit Japanese voice takes first, then time Thai subtitles to actual delivery.
5. **Final gate:** export and play the full MP4; no output should be marked final before playback verification.
